Ode to Joy: piano notes for beginners

Ode to Joy is in C major and runs to 8 bars. The whole melody fits under five fingers without moving your hand once. There are three levels here: the melody on its own, the melody with one bass note per bar, and both hands together. Before you start

Common mistakes on Ode to Joy

  1. Moving the hand. The entire tune sits under one five-finger position.
  2. Getting the dotted rhythm in bars four and eight wrong. It is long, then short.
  3. Playing it too fast. It is a hymn, not a race.

The practice room highlights the bars you keep fluffing and offers to loop them, so you do not have to spot these yourself.
